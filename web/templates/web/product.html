Lao Gan Ma Chili Sauce with Chicken, also known as Tao Huabi, is a seasoning oil that elevates the flavour and takes taste buds on a savory culinary adventure. This world-renowned condiment originates from Guizhou and has gained international acclaim for its unique blend of crispy texture, deep umami, and fragrant heat.

The star ingredient in Lao Gan Ma Chicken Chili Sauce is the carefully selected high-quality chicken, skillfully diced and fried until crunchy, then infused into rich soybean oil alongside sun-dried red chili peppers, garlic, and fragrant Sichuan peppercorns. This sauce delivers a harmonious balance of crispy bits, savory chicken aroma, and appetizing tingling heat.

Whether you're looking to elevate Himalayan momos and dumplings, stir-fry noodles, noodles, fried rice, or marinate roasted meats, Lao Gan Ma Chicken Chili Sauce brings authentic gourmet depth to every single bite.
