5 High-Protein Organic Lentil Dhal Bowls for Autumn & Winter Comfort

By Chef Kiran & Nutrition Desk October 07, 2026 7 min read

When the British weather turns crisp and damp, nothing warms the spirit quite like a steaming bowl of fragrant, spiced Himalayan dhal served alongside fluffy steamed basmati or warm roti.

1. Creamy Yellow Moong Dhal with Ginger & Cumin

Protein per serving: 18g • Cooking time: 20 mins

Gently split yellow mung beans simmered with fresh minced ginger, turmeric, and sea salt. Finish with a rapid tadka of cumin seeds crackled in grass-fed ghee.

2. Smoky Black Urad Dhal (Maa Ki Dal)

Protein per serving: 24g • Cooking time: 45 mins

Whole black lentils slow-simmered with crushed garlic, whole cardamom, and tomato puree. Incredibly rich in iron, dietary fiber, and natural minerals.

3. Quick Coconut Red Lentil Soup (Masoor Dhal)

No soaking required. Red masoor lentils cook in just 15 minutes, melting down into a velvety stew when finished with a splash of rich coconut milk and fresh lime juice.

4. Himalayan Horsegram (Kulthi) Stamina Broth

Traditionally brewed by high-altitude travelers to sustain energy in freezing temperatures. Horsegram is known in Ayurvedic traditions for its potent metabolic warmth.

5. Panchratna (Five-Jewel) Mixed Lentil Pot

A combination of Toor, Chana, Moong, Masoor, and Urad lentils. Delivers a complete amino acid profile, creating a fulfilling meal for busy working professionals and athletes alike.
