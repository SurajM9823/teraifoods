The Cold-Chain Journey: How We Keep Fresh Organic Vegetables Crisp in the UK

By Terai Logistics & Quality Team October 10, 2026 5 min read

Delivering fragile heirloom greens, crisp spinach, and tender organic herbs across British postal zones without losing crispness requires scientific temperature management at every mile.

1. Pre-Cooling at the Farm Gate

Within 90 minutes of harvest, field heat is quickly removed via forced-air hydro-cooling. This puts cellular respiration into slumber, locking in sugars and vitamin C.

Sustainable Wool & Paper Foil Insulation

We utilize 100% compostable sheep wool and recycled paper bubble-liners that outperform harmful polystyrene boxes by maintaining below 4°C for up to 36 hours.

2. Smart Route Planning & Live Postcode Dispatch

Integrated GPS dispatch links each delivery route with real-time UK traffic data. When you enter your UK postcode at checkout, our dispatch engine routes orders through nearest regional fulfillment centers in London, Birmingham, and Manchester.

3. The "Crisp Guarantee"

If any item arrives compromised or wilted, our 1-click replacement policy credits your basket instantly. Freshness is not just a promise; it is our operational benchmark.
