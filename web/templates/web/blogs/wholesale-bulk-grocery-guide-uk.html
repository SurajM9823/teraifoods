Wholesale Grocery Sourcing for UK Caterers, Restaurants & Event Hosts

By Terai Wholesale & Commercial Team October 03, 2026 4 min read

In an era of rising food inflation, UK hospitality businesses need dependable wholesale suppliers who provide price stability without compromising on quality or traceability.

1. Direct Farm-to-Kitchen Supply Chains

By skipping multiple middleman wholesalers, Terai Food connects commercial food businesses directly with primary farm cooperatives. This ensures restaurant chefs receive fresher harvest dates and extended walk-in cooler shelf life.

Key Commercial Benefits:

  • Custom volume discount brackets starting from 25kg / 50-pack thresholds
  • Consolidated invoicing with 30-day corporate trade accounts
  • Pre-dawn temperature-controlled delivery directly to your kitchen back-door
  • Dedicated B2B account managers reachable by phone and WhatsApp

2. Specialty Himalayan & South Asian Staples

From aged long-grain Basmati rice, premium stone-pressed mustard oil, whole spice sacks (Timur, Fenugreek, Cumin), to seasonal fresh herbs, we supply authentic ingredients that distinguish your dining experience.
